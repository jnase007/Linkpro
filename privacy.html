Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Effective September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LINK Pro (“LINK Pro,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information through this website. It is written for the public site — booking requests, pages, and messages — not as a substitute for the clinic’s patient intake paperwork.

Practice location: 3134 Airway Ave, Costa Mesa, CA 92626. Phone: (949) 465-0770. Privacy requests: concierge@linkmedicalcenter.com.

1. Two kinds of information

Website information is what you send through this site (name, email, phone, message, service interest) plus ordinary technical data from your browser.

Clinical records created when you become a patient — exams, treatment notes, health history — are kept by the practice under applicable health-privacy rules, including HIPAA where it applies. Do not upload charts, imaging, or detailed medical records through the public consultation form. Bring that information to the clinic or use the channel the practice gives you after you are a patient.

2. What we collect on the website

  • Information you type into the consultation form: first and last name, email, phone, service interest, and the message you write.
  • Information you send by email, phone, or Instagram if you contact us that way.
  • Basic technical data: IP address, browser type, device, referring page, and pages viewed. This site does not currently run advertising pixels or a cookie banner. Hosting and the embedded Google Map may set their own cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To answer booking requests and schedule consultations, classes, or courses.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To comply with law, enforce our Terms of Use, or protect patients, staff, and the practice.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We share website information only as needed:

  • Staff and contractors who handle scheduling and communications.
  • Vendors that host the site, email, maps, or similar infrastructure — currently including our web host and Google Maps for the contact-page map.
  • Professional advisors or authorities when required by law or to protect the practice.

League marks, team names, and athlete categories on this site describe the level of work the practice has done. They are not a claim that those organizations operate this website or receive your form data.

5. California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may request to know, delete, or correct personal information we hold about you from this website, and you may tell us not to sell or share it (we do not sell or share it as those terms are used in the CCPA/CPRA). You will not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Email concierge@linkmedicalcenter.com or call (949) 465-0770. We may need to verify who you are before fulfilling a request. An authorized agent may submit a request with proof of authority.

Sensitive health details should not be sent through the public form. Clinical records requests follow the practice’s medical-records process, not this website form.

6. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the site. A parent or guardian should contact us directly if a minor needs care.

7. Retention and security

We keep website inquiries long enough to respond, schedule, and maintain ordinary business records, then delete or archive them according to the practice’s retention schedule and legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No website transmission is perfectly secure — that is another reason not to send full medical files through the public form.

8. Third-party sites

Links to Instagram, maps, video, or other sites are not covered by this policy. Their terms and privacy practices apply once you leave this site.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Effective” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
